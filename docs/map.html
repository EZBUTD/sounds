World Map

Spoken Around the World

Pick a language and the map shades the countries where a speaker community is documented within our data source, darker where more people speak it, with flow lines connecting to the language's modern origin.

Coverage is uneven between languages, so this is best read as "where records exist" rather than "everywhere it is spoken" — see the note below the map.

Reading the map

fewer → more speakers (log scale)
homeland
flow to a community abroad (width = speakers)

Country totals count everyone who speaks the language there, native or learned. Hover a shaded country for figures and official status.

What the shading means — and doesn't. Shading marks a documented speaker community, not ownership and not everyone in the country. Figures mix native and second-language speakers.

Unshaded is not proof of absence. For the 16 languages with the thin coverage we added diaspora communities from national statistics agencies; hover any country to see which source its figure came from. Some languages show only 3–4 communities abroad, which reflects thin records as much as small diasporas.

Sources and caveats. Sound inventories: PHOIBLE 2.0 (CC BY-SA). Coordinates and language classification: Glottolog (CC BY). Speaker counts: Ethnologue 2026 figures as tabulated on Wikipedia (CC BY-SA); Ethnologue itself is paywalled. Country-level speaker communities: Unicode CLDR supplemental data, augmented for thinly-covered languages with US Census ACS 2017–2021 detailed language tables, UK ONS 2021, Statistics Canada 2021, the Australian Bureau of Statistics 2021, and other national agencies — each country's source is shown in its hover tooltip. Basemap: Natural Earth (public domain)
